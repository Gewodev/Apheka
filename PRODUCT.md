# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and self-learners in deep work sessions, usually at night in dim ambient environments. They need a cognitive operating system to manage study sessions, tasks, notes, courses, and music without friction. Primary context: desktop/laptop, extended focus blocks, minimizing context switches.

## Product Purpose

West is a high-performance academic operating system for deep study, focus, task execution, planning, and cognitive workflow management. It replaces scattered tools (Notion, Todoist, Google Calendar, Spotify) with one premium integrated environment.

## Brand Personality

Premium, Minimal, Focused. The voice is direct, technical, and calm. No playful tone, no gamification, no "AI assistant" personality.

## Anti-references

- No generic SaaS / startup-template aesthetics
- No Notion-clone feel (but inspired by Notion Calendar's clarity)
- No gamified or colorful apps (Duolingo, Habitica, etc.)
- No "AI assistant" gimmicks or chatbot UI
- No Bootstrap/Material generic card grids
- No saturated rainbow palettes or neon overload
- No bright UI or hard contrast borders

## Design Principles

1. **Cognitive Silence**: Every element has a functional, spatial, and cognitive purpose. Nothing is decorative. Reduce mental friction.

2. **Single Priority Hierarchy**: One dominant element per view. Nothing visually competes with the primary workflow.

3. **Invisible Complexity**: The platform feels simple despite being advanced internally. The user never sees system complexity.

4. **Enterprise Stability**: Mathematically intentional spacing, consistent typography, predictable layouts. Never broken responsive logic or disconnected components.

5. **Deep Intentionality**: Every pixel feels crafted. The interface communicates precision, focus, authority, clarity, stability, and professionalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Reduced motion support. High readability with proper line heights and contrast. No information conveyed through color alone.
